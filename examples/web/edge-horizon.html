// How it works

A shader instead of a picture

The planet, the dotted grid and the network pulses are drawn by one fragment shader on the visitor's GPU: a payload of about 3.5 KB gzip instead of an image or a video. The animation loops every 128 seconds without a seam, pauses when the hero leaves the screen or the tab is hidden, and shows a still frame when the system asks for reduced motion.

Sources: examples/edge_horizon.timeline.json, examples/edge_horizon_common.glsl, examples/shaders/edge_horizon.glsl.
